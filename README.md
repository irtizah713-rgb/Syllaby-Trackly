SyllabyTrackly

Your educational experience, Under Control.

SyllabyTrackly is a digital hub that enables students to plan, manage and analyse their education and also aids them in focusing on their work to help them meet their educational goals.

It includes educational planning, productivity, gaming, and online collaboration features.

Features

Syllabus
Students can make and control educational topics. Students can then sub-divide these into chapters and topics. Students can view which chapters have been studied, and also define which chapters have been studied, re-studied, practiced and/or numericals practiced. Students can analyse how much of a syllabus they have covered. Students can also analyse educational topic specific data.

Tasks
Students can create, amend and categorise tasks. Students can allocate priorities and set due dates for tasks. Students can analyse both completed and outstanding tasks and also utilise a task reminder function.

Notes
Students can produce and organise notes. Notes can be further categorised by topic and then also by chapter. Students can search for notes, favourite and bookmark notes. Notes will automatically save locally.

Focus Mode
Students can utilise a pomodoro function. Students can individually determine their focus and break periods. Students can analyse previous focus periods. Students can also utilise background noise and switch to silent mode. Students can analyse both daily and weekly focus data.
Gaming
Students can earn experience points (XP) and progress through levels. Students can be awarded badges and can also analyse their educational standings. Students can analyse study streaks. Students can also earn Theme Points. Students can then redeem these within a personalisation store.

Analytics
Students can analyse their general educational attainment. Students can analyse how much time that they spend studying. Students can also analyse productivity. Students can analyse chapter coverage and also analyse task completion.

Attendance
Students can utilise a digital, interactive attendance board. Students can analyse their attendance (present, absent and on holiday), and also analyse what percentage of days they were present. Students can analyse monthly attendance data.

Online Study Rooms
Students can utilise public and private online study rooms. Students can invite friends to rooms. Students can study within themed environments including; Forest, Library and Classroom. Students can co-ordinate group study. Students can analyse real-time online room occupancy. Students can also utilise a text only chat function. Messages will automatically be erased after seven days.

Profile & Personalisation
Students can upload profile images. Students can alter their usernames and add bios. Students can analyse their levels and accomplishments. Students can apply profile picture frames and can also personalise their preferred themes.

Architecture
SyllabyTrackly is structured as a cross-platform digital hub comprising:
1. An android app
2. A student web application
3. An admin dashboard
4. A shared backend API
5. Database and hosting
The architectural design focuses on modularity, off-line educational support, scalability and adaptability.
Objectives
To ease educational planning.
To promote regular study.
To enable students to successfully cover their syllabi.

To improve student’s focus and productivity.
